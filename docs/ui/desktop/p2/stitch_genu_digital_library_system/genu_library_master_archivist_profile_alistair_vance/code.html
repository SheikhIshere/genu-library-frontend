Scholarly Mission & Restoration

For over two decades, my sanctuary labor has anchored upon the chemical preservation of pig-skin bindings, hand-cut movable iron types, and early Flemish astronomical folios. My workshop in Edinburgh balances ancient conservation techniques with digital holographic archiving.

As an elected Guild Elder of Genu Library, I curate open-access digital facsimiles of works previously locked within private monastic crypts. Every entry is indexed with meticulous typographic attribution, ink spectrometry, and watermarked fiber analysis.
